# Games28 Public Growth Checklist

## One-time launch tasks

1. Submit `https://games28.paulzuiderduin.com/sitemap.xml` in Google Search Console.
2. Create `@games28.bsky.social` if available; otherwise use `@games28app.bsky.social`.
3. Add the profile link to the Games28 footer after the account exists. Do not ship a link to an unclaimed account.

## Bluesky launch post

> Games28 is an independent LA 2028 schedule and country-dashboard tracker. Browse sessions in your local time, save a country, and export confirmed sessions to your calendar. Official sources only; no roster predictions. https://games28.paulzuiderduin.com/

## Meaningful post triggers

- An official LA28 schedule version changes.
- A verified country qualification record is published.
- Final entries or draws make country sessions confirmed.
- A calendar or country-dashboard improvement gives visitors a new practical reason to return.

## Targeted outreach template

> Hi [name], I made Games28, a free independent LA 2028 schedule tracker focused on local-time sessions, country dashboards, and calendar exports. It only publishes qualification information linked to official sources. I thought [specific page/resource] might find this useful: [relevant Games28 link]. No need to reply if it is not a fit.

Contact only relevant independent Olympic, sport-calendar, country fan, or planning resources once. Do not buy links, mass-email, or imply official affiliation.

## Monthly review

Spend 15 minutes on Search Console and Umami. Check indexed-page issues, impressions, clicks, search queries, referrers, country opens, calendar exports, and shares. Make one small evidence-based improvement only.
